# Errata — what later rounds found wrong in earlier ones

Every item was found by reading records or code during a later round. Records of rounds 1–2 are not in this repository
(`results/sixaxis/` was not committed), so items that affect them are flagged, not re-scored.

| # | found in | what was wrong | what it affects | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | round 3 | **Updates dated before what they supersede.** Dates were drawn within a session week, so a superseding note could carry the earlier date. | Round 2: 3 of the 9 T4 items and 3 of the 9 T3 items in S1 seeds 123 and 456; 1 T4 and 1 T3 in S2 seed 42. Round 2's update (A2) numbers and stale rates are partly gold error. Round 3: 7 of 65 T4 keys, excluded for every system. | generator fixed; `ground_check()` flags it |
| 2 | round 3 | **Implicit role updates were ambiguous** ("X is now <role>" does not say the old role ended). | Round 3: 8 T4 keys, 2 T3 items, 3 deletion controls; reported as primary vs sensitivity. | wording fixed |

Round 2: Generative Agents' "fooled 0.83" and Graphiti's Haiku-tier 0.83 may be the same artifact. | A4 demoted; rule documented as misfiring |
| 4 | round 5 review | **Mem0 always read 20 memories, not 5.** mem0ai 2.x calls the parameter `top_k`; the adapter passed `limit=5`, which was ignored. | Rounds 1–3: Mem0's rows were labelled as a top-5 system; its accuracy and read-token numbers are for 20 extracted memories.
